- Introduced an additional level of navigation: subcategories. Similar contexts are now a part of the same tab and can be switched between with left/right on a d-pad or left stick
	- Damage Done and Boss Damage Done are now just Damage, with old tabs presented as subcategories
	- Healing Out, Self Healing, and Healing In are now just Healing, with old tabs presented as subcategories
